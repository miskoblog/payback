# Payback demo video: script and shot list (DRAFT for approval)

Format: 1920x1080, ~6.5 min. Voice: Mark (ElevenLabs). Fade colour between beats: the app's `--bg`. Password renders as dots; never shown or spoken.
Every view change is a visible cursor click. "Hero" tool = shown end to end; the other two tools in a module get a quick pass.

## Beat 1: Hook (static screenshot, no motion)  ~25s
SHOT: `screenshot.png` (Postonomous sales page) held completely still.
1. "Postonomous makes your content, scores every draft, and publishes it to six networks. And its own sales page is honest about what it leaves to you."
2. "It says: 'Postonomous doesn't run your ads. It helps you choose what's worth putting money behind.' It says credits refill every month, and unused credits don't carry over. And it says that $500 to $5,000 a month is what businesses typically pay for social media management, not a forecast of what you'll charge."
3. "So four decisions are still yours: how far your credits go, what to charge, where ad money should go, and how far to trust a score. That's what Payback is for."

## Beat 2: Unlock the app  ~25s
SHOT: Payback login gate. Cursor clicks the Password field; password typed at real speed (dots only); cursor clicks Unlock. App opens on How to Use.
1. "This is Payback, a free bonus for Postonomous buyers. It sits behind a simple password gate, and this is the whole access flow: type the password you're given, and click unlock."
2. "Inside, Payback is three modules that follow one thread: credits, to price, to proof. Everything runs in your browser. No accounts, no API keys, nothing to connect."

## Beat 3: CreditMap  ~90s
SHOT 3a: cursor glides to the CreditMap tab and clicks it.
1. "Start with CreditMap, where the question is: where does every credit go before the month starts?"
   (Module pitch) "Postonomous tells you credits don't carry over, but it can only show you the price of each action. CreditMap turns that into a plan for the month."
SHOT 3b (hero, Credit Budget Builder): click "Load sales-page Month A business"; totals show 990 credits; cursor opens "Credit rate card" briefly, closes it; ticks "I use my own AI key for text actions" and the text lines drop to 0; unticks it.
2. "In the Credit Budget Builder, set what one brand gets each month. Here's the sales page's own business: sixty posts, eight images, twenty-five scored posts, and it lands at 990 credits. The rates come straight from the sales page and you can edit every one."
3. "Use your own AI key for text? Tick the box and those lines drop to zero. So you see your real number, not a generic one."
SHOT 3c: click "Brand Split & Plan-Fit" subtab; Corner Café / Bright Smiles / Iron Gym rows visible; Plan fit table shown against the 5,000 / 7,000 / 10,000 pools; slow smooth scroll.
4. "Brand Split splits your pool across clients and shows who comes up short, with the arithmetic against each plan size. It shows the math; it doesn't tell you what to buy."
SHOT 3d: click "Use-It-or-Lose-It Tracker" subtab; click "Use pool from Brand Split"; click "Add entry", pick a brand, credits 400; projected balance shows.
5. "And the Tracker logs what you spend, projects where you'll finish, and flags credits that would expire unused."

## Beat 4: Retainer  ~90s
SHOT 4a: cursor glides to the Retainer tab and clicks it.
1. "Next, Retainer: what do I charge?"
   (Module pitch) "The sales page gives you a $500 to $5,000 band and no way to place yourself inside it. Retainer does that, with your own credit cost and your own time."
SHOT 4b (hero, Pricing Engine): Business type dropdown opens (overlay), click "Dentist"; Market size opens, click a market; Posts 16; Good / Better / Best prices appear.
2. "In the Pricing Engine, pick a business type, market size, networks, posts and video load. You get a Good, Better and Best monthly price, with a floor built from your credit cost and your own time."
3. "That's a quote you can say out loud on Monday, not a guess."
SHOT 4c: click "Margin & Payback Calculator" subtab; click "Use Better price from Pricing Engine"; scenario table for 1 / 5 / 10 clients scrolls into view.
4. "The Margin Calculator takes that price and shows profit per client, how many months to recoup your Postonomous purchase, and the one-, five- and ten-client picture, with your numbers."
SHOT 4d: click "Audit One-Pager" subtab; type agency + prospect names; defaults scores show; average and gap computed; one-page sheet visible.
5. "The Audit One-Pager turns five post scores and one rewrite into a printable findings page for a prospect, with your price on it."

## Beat 5: Proven  ~90s
SHOT 5a: cursor glides to the Proven tab and clicks it.
1. "Last is Proven: where does the ad money go, and did it pay off?"
   (Module pitch) "A WinScore is a strong signal, not a guarantee. Proven helps you act on the signal, then check it against what actually happened."
SHOT 5b (hero, Budget Splitter): defaults show Draft A 41, Draft B 58, Draft C 87 with $500 budget; cursor clicks the budget, edits to $600; dollar split updates, Draft A gets $0 "Fix it first"; click "Send to Boost Ledger".
2. "Give the Budget Splitter your drafts with their scores and a total budget. It holds back a test slice, gives nothing to drafts that need fixing first, and puts the rest behind the posts that clear the bar by the most."
3. "So the money follows the score, with the arithmetic in plain sight."
SHOT 5c: click "Predicted-vs-Actual Calibration Log" subtab; click "Add post" and enter a few scores with results; hit rate by band and suggested cutoffs show; click "Apply suggested cutoffs".
4. "The Calibration Log records each score beside what the post really did, then tells you how well scores track results in your niche, and suggests your own cutoffs."
SHOT 5d: click "Boost Ledger" subtab; the sent post is in the ledger; cost per result by tier visible.
5. "And the Boost Ledger tracks spend and results per boosted post, so you can see whether higher scores were cheaper to promote."

## Beat 6: How it fits with Postonomous  ~20s
SHOT: stays on Proven view (no new action).
1. "Payback doesn't replace anything in Postonomous. It picks up where the page leaves off: CreditMap plans the credits, Retainer prices the service, Proven puts ad money behind what's proven. One thread, credits to price to proof."

## Beat 7: How to Use (fixed)  ~25s
SHOT: cursor clicks "How to Use" tab; slow, readable smooth scroll through the guide.
1. "If you ever get stuck, the How to Use tab walks through every tool step by step, so there's no learning curve to worry about."

## Beat 8: Close on the login gate (static, no motion)  ~20s
SHOT: Payback login page, held completely still.
1. "That's Payback: a free companion that turns your credits and scores into a plan, a price and proof. Grab Postonomous, then use your bonus link and password to unlock Payback. Run your own numbers, and enjoy."

Estimates are "estimate, not a forecast or guarantee" per the app's own language; the video makes no income claims.
